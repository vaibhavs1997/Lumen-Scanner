Lumen for Google Play
=====================

Package name: app.lumen.scanner
Version: 1.0.0 (versionCode 1)
Upload key alias: lumen
Keystore file: stored privately outside this project
Keystore password: stored privately; never commit or document it here
Key password: stored privately; never commit or document it here

What to upload
--------------
Upload lumen-release.aab in Google Play Console → Your app → Production
(or Internal testing first).

lumen-release.apk is only for installing on your own phone to try it.
Do not upload the APK to Play — Play wants the .aab.

Store listing
-------------
App name: Lumen
Short description:
Scan pages with your camera. Clean PDFs from photos — no hardware scanner.

Full description:
Lumen turns your phone into a document scanner.

• Photograph a page with the camera
• Import photos or a PDF
• Auto-detect edges, flatten perspective, and clean the page
• Enhance, black-and-white, grayscale, or photo
• Export a multi-page PDF or a single image

Pages are processed on your device. Lumen does not require an account.

Privacy policy URL
------------------
After you publish the Lumen website from Grok, use:

  https://YOUR-APP-HOST/privacy

Play Console will not accept the listing without a public privacy policy URL.

Content rating
--------------
Complete Google’s questionnaire. This app is a productivity scanner,
not a social network, and does not share user content online.

Data safety
-----------
No data is collected or shared by Lumen servers. Camera and files are
used only on-device to create scans you save or share.

Keep the private upload key and its password secure. Release builds read them
from LUMEN_KEYSTORE_FILE, LUMEN_KEYSTORE_PASS, and LUMEN_KEY_PASS.
